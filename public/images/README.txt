HOW TO ADD YOUR PERSONAL PHOTOS:
==================================

You can replace any of the images in this folder (`/public/images/`) with your own:

1. `avatar.png` - Your circular profile picture / avatar.
   - Recommended: Square aspect ratio (500x500 or larger), PNG or JPG.
   - Name it `avatar.png` (or update personal.avatar in `/data/portfolio.ts`).

2. `ram-photo.jpg` - Your candid snapshot / polaroid photo.
   - Recommended: Landscape or square aspect ratio (e.g. 800x600 or 600x600).
   - Name it `ram-photo.jpg`.

3. `album-art.jpg` - Background for the music / now-playing card.
   - Recommended: 16:9 or 1:1 image.

4. `resume.pdf` - (Optional) Place your updated resume PDF here so the Resume icon can download it directly!
